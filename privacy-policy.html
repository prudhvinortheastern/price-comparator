Privacy Policy This extension collects limited user information such as name and email address during registration in order to provide personalized price comparison services. The extension also reads product information (such as product title and price) from supported e-commerce webpages to perform price comparison. This information is only used for the functionality of the extension and is not sold, shared, or transferred to third parties. Product metadata may be sent to a secure backend service for price comparison analysis. We do not collect sensitive personal data such as financial information, passwords, or location data. All collected information is used strictly to improve the functionality of the price comparison service.
